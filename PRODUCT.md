# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Private tutors managing their own teaching business — students, class schedules, hour tracking, and billing. They are subject-matter experts, not software operators. Their context: juggling multiple students across weekly schedules, tracking remaining class hours, and keeping student records organized. The primary workflow is checking today's schedule, managing student details, and confirming completed classes.

## Product Purpose

TutorPal replaces scattered spreadsheets, calendars, and notes with a focused management tool purpose-built for private tutors. It centralizes student records, class scheduling with automatic hour deduction, and LINE messaging integration. Success means a tutor opens the app, immediately sees what matters today, and manages their business without friction.

## Brand Personality

Warm, approachable, professional. The voice is competent without being cold — a trusted assistant, not a corporate dashboard. Three words: **warm, precise, calm**.

Emotional goal: confidence that nothing is falling through the cracks. The interface should feel like a well-organized desk, not a spreadsheet.

## Anti-references

- **Corporate / spreadsheet-like**: Dense data tables, monochrome UI, overwhelming grids of numbers. TutorPal should feel human, not like accounting software.
- **SaaS-cream generic AI**: Warm beige backgrounds, soft shadows, gradient accents — the saturated default of 2026. TutorPal has its own identity.
- **Gamified / childish**: Bright emoji, excessive color, patronizing micro-interactions. Tutors are professionals.

## Design Principles

1. **One task at a time**: Every screen has a clear primary action. Don't competing for attention.
2. **Show the human, not the data**: Student names and faces first, IDs and metadata secondary.
3. **Earn the border**: Every visual boundary must create hierarchy. No decorative lines.
4. **Progressive disclosure**: Default to the essential; reveal complexity only when the user seeks it.
5. **Respect the rhythm**: Consistent spacing and typography create a calm, scannable experience.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA compliance: minimum 4.5:1 contrast for body text, 3:1 for large text
- Keyboard navigation throughout
- Screen reader support with semantic HTML and ARIA labels
- Reduced motion alternatives for all animations
- Color is never the sole indicator of state — paired with text/icons
